Flutter 3.47.5. Each run of `apps/sample`'s probe test records two 5 s
windows on the calibration screen: **animated** (a box turns and slides)
and **still** (nothing changes). A plant, when built in, runs only in the
animated window, so the still window is its control. Semantics are off
unless stated.

Values are means over runs, with ranges in brackets. `B` is the frame
budget, 8.33 ms at 120 Hz, so a 5 s window should hold
5 × 120 = 600 frames. "Gaps" are skipped frame numbers.

## Hitch time with and without missed vsyncs

Means over runs, hitch time in ms with the ratio in ms/s in brackets.
Each ratio divides by its own rendering time. Overrun is
`max(UI, raster) − B` summed over late frames.

- **A:** overrun only (decision record 0001).
- **B:** overrun + missed vsyncs × `B`.
- **C:** the larger of overrun and missed vsyncs × `B`.
- **D:** as B, with each gap also explained by the frame's own raster
  time.
- **E:** as D, with a gap also explained by the raster time of the frame

## Recorder overhead

Ten profile runs per arm on each phone, alternating the recorder on and
off, with `integration_test/m2_overhead_test.dart` and
`test_driver/overhead_driver.dart`. Each run traces the animated and the
still window from the VM timeline, and `TimelineSummary` from
`flutter_driver` gives the p99 frame build and raster time. Times are in
ms; "diff" is on − off.

| Phone | Window | Build p99 on | Build p99 off | Diff | Raster p99 on | Raster p99 off | Diff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S24 | animated | 0.83 [0.79..0.89] | 0.89 [0.78..1.38] | -0.06 | 2.56 [1.88..3.39] | 3.42 [1.89..9.97] | -0.86 |
| S24 | still | 0.73 | 0.68 | +0.05 | 2.20 | 2.69 | -0.49 |
| iPhone | animated | 0.51 [0.34..1.57] | 0.46 | +0.05 | 0.55 | 0.57 | -0.02 |
| iPhone | still | 0.45 [0.21..1.24] | 0.35 | +0.10 | 0.65 [0.39..2.15] | 0.52 | +0.13 |

Medians differ by under 0.07 ms in every row. No difference stands out
from the noise: each is smaller than the spread between runs of one arm,
and the sign goes both ways. The largest differences are in S24 raster
p99, where the runs with the recorder on were faster, by 0.86 and
0.49 ms. In the animated window one "off" run at 9.97 ms drives it:
without that run the "off" mean is (34.2 − 9.97) ÷ 9 = 2.69 ms, against
2.56 with the recorder on. The largest rise, +0.13 ms of raster time on
the iPhone still window, comes from one "on" run and is 1.6% of `B`
(0.13 ÷ 8.33). That fits the callback cost of 2 to 3 µs per frame in the
clean table above.

Under tracing, the summary counted 441 to 454 frames per window on the
S24 and 388 to 398 on the iPhone, not 600, in both arms alike. Why is not
known: tracing may cost frames, or the trace may miss some. The
comparison holds because both arms lose the same, but these p99s describe
traced runs, not the untraced runs the recorder normally sees.

## Getting results off the phones

- **Android:** `adb logcat`. At about 1,300 one-frame lines per run,
  logcat dropped about 400 lines without warning. Packing 20 frames per
  line fixed it.
- **iOS, profile:** Dart `print` reaches the `flutter drive` transcript
  through the VM service. No line was lost.
- **iOS, release:** `idevicesyslog -u <udid> -p Runner` from
  libimobiledevice, with no root. Both release runs got every line.
  `xcrun devicectl device process launch --console` gets nothing in
  release, and `log collect --device` needs root.

## How the runs were driven

- Profile: `flutter drive --profile --no-dds --keep-app-running`. Without
  `--keep-app-running`, `flutter drive` uninstalls the app when it stops
  (`packages/flutter_tools/lib/src/drive/drive_service.dart`, `stop()`).
  On iOS that removed the only app signed by the developer account, so
  the phone asked to trust the developer again before every run.
- Release: `flutter drive` refused release builds, so release runs were
  launched with `flutter run --release --no-resident` on Android and
  through `xcrun devicectl` on iOS, and their logs read as above.
- Xcode must be closed during iOS runs, or `flutter drive` launches
  through it and stalls.
