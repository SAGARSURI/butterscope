# M4 measurements

The device data behind decision record
[0004](../decisions/0004-m4-sample-findings.md), taken on 2026-10-06 on
the same Galaxy S24 and iPhone 17 Pro as M2, with Flutter 3.47.5 in
profile mode.

## Method

`apps/sample/integration_test/m4_probe_test.dart` opens each sample
screen in turn and records one 5 s window with M2's recorder while a
scripted action runs: flings on Feed, Gallery and Detail, typing on
Search, and waiting on Activity and Inbox, whose streams do the work. M2's
calibration probe records the calibration screen's animated window, where
its plants run. `tool/m4/run_probe.sh` runs both.

Frames lost = the window's wall-clock length × the screen's rate − the
frames recorded, as a share of the frames expected. "Observed" is the
observed refresh rate (DESIGN section 4); "n/a" means too few smooth
frames to tell. Semantics are off in every run below.

Each batch started with one uncounted clean warm-up run, except the
S24's screen-plant, drift-check and first calibration batches, which
came before the warm-up was added. On the S24 each run started at thermal status 0 with the battery
at 38 °C or less, power saving off and Stay awake on.

The margin is a plant's lowest run less the mean of its screen's clean
runs on the same phone. Decision 1 of 0004 asks for 10% to 60% lost and a
margin of at least 10 points.

## Ordinary tests

`tool/m4/run_tests.sh`, with the final costs: the clean build and each of
the six screen plants passed on both phones, 7 of 7 runs each.

On the iPhone, the unplanted screens in these runs lost under 1.3%. In
the S24's batch, unplanted Search lost 2.9 to 9.0%, as its early clean
runs did.

The S24's plant runs came before the warm-up run and the stopping of the
previous run's app were added, and its clean means come from the later
drift check. So the S24's margins compare runs taken under different
conditions. The worst unplanted Search run in the plant batch lost 9.0%,
4.5 points over Search's clean mean of 4.51. If every plant run carried
that much extra loss, the smallest S24 margin, `sync_decode`'s 15.3,
would still be 15.3 − 4.5 = 10.8, over decision 1's 10 points.

The S24's `slow_raster` lost 39.85, 41.01 and 46.18% in an earlier
batch, with the battery at 34 to 35 °C; the runs above came after a
cool-down, at 33 °C.

`listener_decode` loses about 17% per frame its job costs, since it
fires every 50 ms, 6 frames at 120 Hz. At 3 frame budgets a job costs 2
or 3 frames, 33% or 50%, and keeps to one within a run: one earlier
iPhone run lost 33.34%.

## Performance overlay

`tool/m4/run_overlay.sh` took each screenshot 3 s into the screen's
scripted action, from the Mac, on both phones. The raster chart's
average, or its bursts where the cost comes and goes:

| Screen | Plant | S24 clean | S24 planted | iPhone clean | iPhone planted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feed | `raster_clip` | 3.0 ms | 15.9 ms | 1.3 ms | 3.2 ms |
| Detail | `gpu_blur` | 2.9 ms | bursts to 98 ms | 1.1 ms | bursts to 22 ms |
| Calibration | `slow_raster` | 1.6 ms | 14.0 ms | 0.7 ms | 0.6 ms |
| Calibration | `backdrop_blur` | 1.6 ms | 14.2 ms | 0.7 ms | 14.8 ms |
| Calibration | `gpu_heavy` | 1.6 ms | 13.7 ms | 0.7 ms | 0.1 ms |

On the S24 every planted chart went over the 8.3 ms budget line. On the
iPhone, `raster_clip`, `slow_raster` and `gpu_heavy` stayed under it,
although the probe lost 43 to 47% of frames with each.

The planted Feed values and the S24's `gpu_blur` come from the committed
script. The other cells come from an earlier draft of it, which took the
screenshots the same way, except the iPhone's clean Feed, which was
taken by hand.
